## Overview

HP3 is the third prototype in my Hotzenplotz Minigame series.

The goal is to explore **environment interaction** rather than combat or story.

The player takes the role of **Petrusilius Zwackelmann**, trying to maintain his magical castle while interacting with objects, rooms and magical systems.

---

- 🏰 Interactive castle
- 🧙 Environmental magic
- 🌿 Garden and resource management
- 📚 Discoverable secrets
